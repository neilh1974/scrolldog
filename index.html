Scrolldog makes your scrolling visible.

A tiny pixel dog shows up as you keep scrolling. The longer the session goes, the more dramatic it gets.

Download the actual extension files.

The download contains the current manifest, content script, popup, styles, and pixel art icons used by Scrolldog.

Download Scrolldog files
  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Open Chrome extensions and choose load unpacked.
  3. Visit a supported feed site and scroll until the dog appears.
